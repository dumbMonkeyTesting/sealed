how it works

Three moves. Real SOL only crosses the border at the first and the last txn. Everything between them is encrypted.

01

shield

Deposit SOL. The vault mints the same number of cSOL. No proof yet: the program credits only what you sent.

send private

Your browser proves the amount fits and the math balances. The chain checks the proof, not the number.

03

unshield

Withdraw cSOL back to plain SOL. That amount is public again. The encryption keys come from a signature, so there is nothing extra to store.
